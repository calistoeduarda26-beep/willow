Questions, answered

What is in the box?

The Willow Go set includes two pumps, flanges and sizing inserts, 5 oz containers, container backs, splashguards, diaphragms, duckbill valves and two USB charging cables. A USB power adapter is not included. Exact contents are confirmed on your order page.

How do I charge it?

With the included USB cables, using any standard 5 V, 1–2 A USB power source. The power adapter is not included.

How does the app work?

The Willow app connects to the pumps so you can start, stop and adjust suction from your phone. It is available for iPhone and Android.

How much milk do the containers hold?

The included containers hold 5 oz each. 7 oz containers are sold separately by the manufacturer.
